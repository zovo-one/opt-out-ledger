Save the details opt-out forms keep asking for.

Every broker form wants the same things. Your name, city, state, maybe an email or phone. Enter them once here and the extension pre-fills each form when you click Fill. You review and submit every time.

Only first and last name are required, because the forms ask for them. Everything else is optional, and you can skip this whole step and set it up later in Settings.
